1 · US

US intelligence chief defends Trump’s comments calling Putin his ‘friend’

Jay Clayton was pressed on Trump’s characterization of Putin during media appearances on Sunday Jay Clayton, the US director of national intelligence, said he trusts Donald Trump “completely” after the US president called Vladimir Putin “a friend” during their recent meeting in China. Clayton made the remarks on Sunday, where he was pressed during media appearances on Trump’s warm characterization of the Russian president – who is viewed as a chief US adversary – following his trilateral summit with Putin and China’s Xi Jinping. After a backlash at home and aboard, Trump doubled down on Saturday and posted a photo of himself and Putin shaking hands with the caption “My Friend.”
